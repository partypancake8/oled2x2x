overview

what is this?

Four 128×64 OLED displays multiplexed through a TCA9548A, all on a single I²C bus alongside a MAX17048 battery gauge and SHT4x temperature/humidity sensor.

Two cycling screen sets rotate every 15 seconds — the first shows practical info, the second is a bit more fun.

lesson learned

no wifi? no problem.

The ESP32-S3's internal RTC keeps ticking after a single NTP sync. Time stays accurate without any network connection — important for a device that might be shown offline.

why does this exist

a display that's actually yours

E-ink displays are cool. This is cooler — color, fast refresh, and every screen is independently controllable. Don't like the clock? Replace it with a to-do count. Add a fifth screen. Reroute a mux channel. The firmware is simple and easy to modify.

built for people who want a living desk widget — future additions are a new .ino function away.
